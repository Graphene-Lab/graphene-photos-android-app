# Graphene Photos 1.1.9

This release improves photo access when choosing folders to sync.

## Folder selection and photo permissions

- Opening **Choose folder** on the sync screen or **Choose Folders** in Profile now requests photo access when needed, without requiring you to start a sync first.
- Fix misleading **No photo folders found** messages when Photos has not been granted access to the photo library.
- Refresh available folders after granting photo access or changing permissions in Android settings.
- Show clear guidance when access is denied or limited, with a way to manage permissions even when no folders are visible.
- Handle Android 13 photo permissions and Android 14's selected-photo access.
- Preserve your existing folder selections when photo permissions change.
- Add translated permission guidance in Spanish, French, Italian, and Russian.

## Release details

- Android version: **1.1.9**
- Version code: **21**
- Google Play track: **internal testing**
- The GitHub APK is Google's signed universal APK, checked against Photos' Play app signing certificate and package/version before attachment.
